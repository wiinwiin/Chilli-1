Chilli's Heart

"Chilli's heart is for the company directors who lie awake at night wondering if they made the right hire. For the job seekers who've been ghosted one too many times. For everyone in between who deserves better than the industry has been giving them."

— Pina Stojko, Founder

Dear People Leader,

We know what it's like to spend three months interviewing candidates, finally make an offer, watch them start — and then quietly wonder if you got it wrong. We know the sinking feeling when someone you trusted starts underperforming. We know how much time, energy, and money goes into a hire that doesn't work out.

We also know what it's like on the other side of the desk. The job seeker who's been through five rounds of interviews, told they're the frontrunner, and then met with silence. The professional who's been sent to a role completely misrepresented by an agency that just wanted to fill a quota. The candidate who felt like a transaction, not a person.

The Chilli Group exists because both of these experiences are unacceptable — and because we know they don't have to happen.

When we work with a client, we take responsibility for the outcome. Not just the placement — the outcome. We don't consider our job done when someone starts. We consider it done when they're thriving six months later and you're glad you made the call.

When we work with a candidate, we tell them the truth. About the role. About the culture. About whether it's actually the right move for them. Even when that means we lose the placement.

That's what Chilli's heart means. It means we care — genuinely, inconveniently, stubbornly — about getting it right.

Sincerely,

Chilli ❤

01

Trust

Trust isn't a value you declare — it's one you earn, through consistent action, honest communication, and the willingness to say things that are hard to hear.

How This Shows Up

  • ✓ We tell clients when a brief is unrealistic — even if it costs us the engagement
  • ✓ We tell candidates the truth about a role, including the difficult parts
  • ✓ We never present a candidate we wouldn't personally vouch for
  • ✓ We are transparent about fees, timelines, and challenges from day one
  • ✓ We return every call and email — even when we don't have good news

In practice: A client briefed us for a role with a salary band that was 20% below market rate. We told them. They pushed back. We showed them the data. They adjusted the band. We filled the role in 11 days with a candidate who's still there three years later.

We are not in the business of effort. We are in the business of results. Good intentions are not enough — execution is what matters, and we hold ourselves to the same standards we'd expect from any candidate we place.

How This Shows Up

  • ✓ We commit to specific timelines — and we hit them
  • ✓ Our shortlists are concise because we do the hard work of eliminating before you see anyone
  • ✓ We measure our success at 12 months retention, not at placement
  • ✓ We debrief after every search — what worked, what didn't, what we'd do differently
  • ✓ We back our placements with a 12-month replacement guarantee

In practice: 94% of our permanent placements are still in role at 12 months. Industry average is closer to 70%. We know, because we track it.

03

Courage

The recruitment industry rewards compliance — telling clients what they want to hear, sending candidates who tick boxes but don't rock the boat. Courage is our antidote to that. We say what needs to be said.

How This Shows Up

  • ✓ We challenge briefs when we think they're wrong — respectfully, but directly
  • ✓ We recommend candidates who might surprise you, backed by our reasoning
  • ✓ We walk away from engagements we don't believe we can fulfil well
  • ✓ We give feedback to candidates that other agencies soften into meaninglessness
  • ✓ We advocate for candidates when we believe the client is making a mistake

In practice: A client wanted to reject a candidate based on a short gap in their CV. We pushed back, explained the context, and asked them to interview anyway. That candidate became their General Manager and has been in the role for four years.

Recruitment is a serious business that doesn't have to be a joyless one. We bring genuine enthusiasm to what we do, and we believe that working with good people — whether placing them or partnering with them — should be an experience worth having.

How This Shows Up

  • ✓ We celebrate placements properly — they matter, and so should how we mark them
  • ✓ We create an environment where our team loves what they do
  • ✓ We bring energy and genuine enthusiasm to every brief
  • ✓ We treat every candidate interaction as an opportunity to make someone's day better
  • ✓ We laugh. A lot. Life's too short to work with people who don't.

In practice: We've had clients tell us their experience working with us was "genuinely fun" — which we take as the highest possible compliment in an industry that's famous for being neither.

05

Connection

The best hires happen when there's a genuine connection — between candidate and role, between person and culture, between the new hire and the team they're joining. Our job is to create the conditions for that connection to happen.

How This Shows Up

  • ✓ We build long-term relationships, not transactional engagements
  • ✓ We stay in touch with placed candidates — they're part of the Chilli network forever
  • ✓ We introduce clients to each other when we see synergies
  • ✓ We remember the details — the names, the promotions, the personal milestones
  • ✓ Our average client relationship is 7 years. That's not an accident.

In practice: Over 60% of our new client engagements come from direct referrals from existing clients. Connection isn't a value we aspire to — it's one that's already working.
