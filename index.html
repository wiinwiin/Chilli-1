How It Works

Our Process is Simple. Our Results Are Not.

We've refined our recruitment process over 18 years to remove friction, reduce time-to-hire, and improve retention at every step.

01

Deep Discovery

We spend real time learning your business — your culture, your team dynamics, your leadership style, and what's made previous hires succeed or fail.

02

Targeted Search

We tap our curated talent network and run a structured headhunting campaign targeting both active and passive candidates who match your brief precisely.

03

Rigorous Screening

Every candidate goes through behavioural interviews, reference checks, and culture-fit assessments before we ever put their name in front of you.

04

Shortlist & Present

You receive a concise shortlist of three to five exceptional candidates — with detailed write-ups, not just CVs — so every conversation is worth your time.

05

Placement & Aftercare

We manage the offer process, support onboarding, and check in at 30, 60, and 90 days. Our job isn't done when a contract is signed — it's done when your hire is thriving.
