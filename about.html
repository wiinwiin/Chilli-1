How We Started

From Adelaide to Australia

The Chilli Group was founded in 2008 with one clear frustration: most recruitment agencies were treating hiring like a numbers game. Send more CVs. Fill more roles. Collect more fees. The actual fit — the culture, the retention, the human side of it — was an afterthought.

Our founder Pina Stojko set out to build something different: an agency small enough to actually know their clients, stubborn enough to say no to the wrong candidate, and dedicated enough to measure success years after placement — not days.

What started as a single-desk operation in Adelaide has grown into a national agency with consultants operating across five cities. But the fundamentals haven't changed. We still work the same way we always have: personally, transparently, and relentlessly.

Meet the Founder

Pina Stojko

Founder & Managing Director

Pina has been recruiting for over 20 years. Before founding The Chilli Group, she watched the industry operate in ways that consistently frustrated both clients and candidates — high volume, low care, and a revolving door of placements that rarely stuck.

She started Chilli with a simple premise: if you take the time to truly understand a business and truly understand a candidate, the right match isn't that hard to find. The industry just doesn't take that time because time isn't billable at volume.

Today Pina oversees a team of specialist consultants across Australia, still personally involved in senior placements and still measuring success the same way she always has: at 12 months, not day one.
