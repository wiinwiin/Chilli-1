What Is Bulk Hiring?

Volume Without
Compromising Quality

Bulk hiring is any situation where you need to place multiple people into similar roles within a compressed timeframe. It typically applies when:

  • ✓ You're opening a new site or facility
  • ✓ You've won a new contract that requires rapid staffing
  • ✓ You're expanding an existing team by 20%+
  • ✓ You have seasonal peaks requiring workforce ramp-up
  • ✓ You're standing up a new business unit or department

Why It's Different

Why Standard Recruitment
Doesn't Scale

Volume hiring breaks most traditional recruitment models. The normal process — brief, search, screen, present, interview — doesn't have the throughput for 50 hires. Without dedicated infrastructure, quality falls, timelines blow out, and you end up with high attrition.

  • ✓ We assign a dedicated project team (not a single consultant)
  • ✓ We build structured assessment centres for efficiency
  • ✓ We maintain quality gates at every stage
  • ✓ We provide weekly reporting and milestones

Our Process

The Chilli Bulk Hiring Approach

A structured 5-phase project methodology refined across multiple large-scale workforce expansion projects.

Phase 1

Project Brief & Planning

We conduct a deep discovery session — headcount targets, role profiles, timelines, site readiness, onboarding capacity. We design the project plan before a single candidate is approached.

Phase 2

Talent Pipeline Build

We activate multiple sourcing channels simultaneously — advertising, headhunting, our existing network, and targeted outreach. We build a pipeline 3x the target headcount to account for attrition.

Phase 3

Assessment & Screening

Case Study

How We Hired 73 People in 3 Weeks

Client: National 3PL Company
Location: Brisbane distribution centre
Roles: Warehouse operators, team leaders, dispatch staff
Timeline: 21 days from brief to first day

The Challenge

The client won a major new logistics contract and needed to stand up a 73-person warehouse team within 3 weeks. The site was under construction. Onboarding facilities were minimal. Most agencies told them it couldn't be done at quality.

What We Did

We assigned a 4-person project team, built a dedicated assessment centre model, ran group screening sessions 3 times per week, and maintained daily progress reporting to the client's GM. We sourced from 6 channels simultaneously.
