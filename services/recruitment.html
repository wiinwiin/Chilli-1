Our Process

How Chilli Recruitment Works

A structured 6-step process refined over 18 years and 3,300+ placements.

01

Brief & Discovery (Day 1–2)

We meet you in person or via video. We learn your culture, team, leadership style, and what's made previous hires succeed and fail. We ask harder questions than most agencies.

02

Search Strategy (Day 2–3)

We design a tailored search strategy — which networks to tap, which companies to target, which passive candidates to approach. You'll see and approve the strategy before we begin.

03

Active Search (Day 3–7)

We headhunt, outreach, and screen. We run structured behavioural interviews and reference-check all candidates before they reach your shortlist. No padding.

04

Shortlist Presentation (Day 7–10)

You receive 3–5 candidates. Each comes with our written assessment, key strengths, areas to probe in interview, and our honest recommendation. These are all worth meeting.

05

Interview Support & Selection (Day 10–18)

We support your interview process, provide candidate coaching, manage the debrief, and help you structure the offer. We're in the room (metaphorically) for every step.

06

Placement & Aftercare (Ongoing)

Offer negotiated. Contract signed. Then we check in at 30, 60, and 90 days. If something's off, we want to know before it becomes a problem. Your hire is backed for 12 months.
