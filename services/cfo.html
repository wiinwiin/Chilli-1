The Role

What a Fractional CFO Actually Does

A fractional CFO is not a bookkeeper, not an accountant, and not an auditor. They're a strategic finance leader who makes decisions and drives outcomes.

📈 Financial Strategy

Sets financial direction aligned to your business goals. Builds the models that let you make confident decisions about growth, investment, and risk.

📋 Reporting & Visibility

Creates dashboards and reporting systems that give you real visibility into margin, cashflow, and performance — in language you can actually use.

💰 Cash & Capital Management

Manages working capital, identifies cashflow risks before they become crises, and structures financing when needed.

🤝 Stakeholder Relationships

Manages your bank, investors, and board from a finance perspective — preparing presentations, responding to queries, and maintaining confidence.

🔍 Risk & Compliance

Builds internal controls, identifies financial risks, and ensures your finance function meets regulatory and compliance requirements.

🎯 M&A & Growth Support

Supports acquisitions, capital raises, and major transactions — providing the financial rigour that investors and acquirers expect.

Engagement Options

Three Ways to Work With Us

We structure engagements to match your need — not our preference.

Ongoing Retainer

Best for: Growing SMEs needing consistent strategic finance support

A fixed number of days per month with your dedicated CFO. Attend leadership meetings, manage ongoing reporting, and be available for decisions as they arise.

  • ✓ 1–4 days per month
  • ✓ Same CFO each time
  • ✓ Flexible up or down with notice
Most Popular

Project-Based

Best for: Specific initiatives with a defined start and end

A scoped engagement with clear deliverables — financial model build, budget rebuild, board pack creation, due diligence support, or system implementation.

  • ✓ Fixed scope and fee
  • ✓ Typically 4–12 weeks
  • ✓ Clear deliverables agreed upfront

Interim Placement

Best for: Gap cover or transformation mandates

Full-time or near-full-time engagement for a defined period — covering a vacancy, managing a crisis, or leading a transformation before a permanent hire is made.

  • ✓ Full availability for the period
  • ✓ Smooth handover to permanent CFO
  • ✓ Can assist with permanent hire
